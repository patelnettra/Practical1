Our Mission

Helping Hands is a non-governmental organization dedicated to improving the lives of underprivileged communities through education, healthcare, food distribution, and environmental activities.

Ongoing Projects

Education for All

Providing educational materials and learning opportunities to children from economically weaker sections.

Food Distribution

Distributing nutritious food packages to families in need.

Clean Environment

Organizing tree plantation and cleanliness campaigns in local communities.

Volunteer Opportunities

You can support our organization by volunteering in:

  • Teaching children
  • Food distribution programs
  • Tree plantation activities
  • Community awareness programs

Interested volunteers can contact us to join our programs.
